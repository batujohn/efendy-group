Maydanoz — Parsley

Produce-driven meze from the Aegean coast.

Maydanoz draws from the vegetable gardens, olive groves and fishing villages of Turkey's Aegean coast — a cuisine built on olive oil, dairy, fire and restraint. Every plate begins with a single ingredient at its seasonal peak.
