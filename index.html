About Efendy Group

Rooted in tradition,
shaped by innovation.

Founded in 2007 in Sydney by Somer Sivrioğlu, Efendy Group has grown into a celebrated hospitality collective with five distinctive brands.

Inspired by the rich heritage of Turkish cuisine and the spirit of Anatolian hospitality, our mission is to share Türkiye's diverse culinary culture with both local and international audiences. Each brand reflects a unique interpretation of this vision — combining seasonal, locally sourced ingredients with traditional techniques and contemporary flair.

Driven by a dynamic team led by Berk Cemail and Fatih Kulle, Efendy Group proudly represents the evolving face of Turkish gastronomy on the global stage.

Efendy İstanbul

Est. 2007

Born in Australia in 2007 as the inaugural brand of the group, Efendy draws inspiration from the diverse flavors of Anatolian cuisine. Our menu is a testament to the culture of complete sharing — traditional recipes prepared using ingredients cultivated on these historic lands and cooked with time-honoured techniques.

23 Barangaroo Avenue, Sydney

Anason

Est. 2016

Inspired by the modern meyhanes of Istanbul, Anason merges traditional meze culture with the beverages of Turkey — rakı and local wines — in the cosmopolitan setting of Barangaroo. RCNSW Best Specialty Restaurant 2022–2025.

Discover
50 Carrington St, Sydney CBD

Maydanoz

Est. 2021

Maydanoz is an Aegean Turkish bar and restaurant paying homage to the vegetarian and olive oil-driven casual meze bars of the coast. Produce-driven, wood-fired, and guided by a "nose to tail, no waste" philosophy. SMH 1 Hat 2024 & 2025.

Discover
Sydney Fish Market, Pyrmont

Hamsi Taverna

Est. 2026

Named after Türkiye's Black Sea anchovy, Hamsi is inspired by the seaside taverns dotted along the Aegean coast. Charcoal is the main ingredient — skewered sardines, kataifi-wrapped king prawns, sujuk-butter scallops — alongside a fish sandwich set to the Bosphorus.
