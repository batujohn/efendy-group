About Anason

A modern meyhane,
on the water.

"Anason is a love letter to the meyhane — the raucous, generous, impossibly warm Turkish tavern. We brought it to the waterfront and gave it a Sydney soul."

Somer Sivrioğlu — Founder, Efendy Group
The Restaurant

A table for every kind of evening.

Anason opened in 2016 on Barangaroo waterfront, bringing the ritual of the Istanbul meyhane to Sydney Harbour. The concept is simple: fill the table, pour the rakı, let the meze keep coming. The rest takes care of itself.

We have been named RCNSW Best Specialty Restaurant four years running and hold a SMH Good Food Guide recommendation. But the accolades have never changed the way we work — with warmth, speed, and genuine generosity.

Anason seats 120 inside and on the terrace. Walk-ins are always welcome. The harbour is always right outside.

The Food

Meze as a way of life.

Our menu draws from the length of the Turkish table — cold meze, hot meze, charcoal dishes, and seasonal specials. Everything is designed for sharing. Nothing arrives on schedule. That's the point.

We work with Sydney's best producers and import key ingredients direct from Turkey. From creamy haydari and crispy sigara böreği to chargrilled lamb and fresh-caught hamsi, the menu changes with the season but the spirit stays the same.

Dietary requirements are handled with care. Please let us know when you book.

The Drink — Şerefe

Rakı is the reason
you stay.

We carry the most comprehensive list of Turkish rakı in Australia — from the classic Yeni Rakı to small-batch artisan expressions from Çeşme and Thrace. Our sommelier curates a natural wine list with a strong focus on Anatolian producers.

Cocktails are seasonal, spirit-forward, and always anchored to the Mediterranean. The Anason Sour has been on the menu since opening night. We have never taken it off.

Our People

Warmth is the job description.

"Every person on the Anason floor understands that hospitality is not service — it is generosity. It is reading the table, anticipating the need, and making every guest feel like the most important person in the room. Because in that moment, they are."
