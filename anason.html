About Anason

A meyhane spirit
on the harbour.

Anason opened in 2016 on the Barangaroo waterfront, bringing the ritual of the Istanbul meyhane to Sydney Harbour.

Named for anise — the base of rakı — the concept is simple: fill the table, pour the rakı, let the meze keep coming. RCNSW Best Specialty Restaurant four years running.
